## Scope and Baseline

- **Selection**: No matching P05 feature exists among the inspected P01–P04 features. Create one feature for P05 and select it instead of P04. Preserve prior artifacts, requirement/task IDs, decisions and progress.
- **Current evidence**: Intake revision `9a7e13f`, actual branch `main`. Cutting/production screens and their work/batch/dialog actions still use browser-local operational state, fixed worker names and independent quantity segments. Legacy task selection can fall back to a different segment/piece; batch actions silently omit ineligible selections. Production repair copy claims a cutting bypass. - **Prerequisite evidence**: P04's final T047 ledger records all 47 tasks complete and local phase acceptance on November 7, 2026. Current source supplies persisted submitted pieces, initial approval/release, issue reporting/decisions, factory-safe specifications and command-result observation. This is inspected source plus reused recorded evidence, not a fresh execution of those tests. Reconcile current prerequisite interfaces and evidence before implementation.
- **Deliverables**: Both departments' dashboard/counts, incoming/in-progress/returned/completed/activity reads, filters, grouped/flat views, order/task detail and aliases; exact-piece receipt/start/continuation/completion/transfer; individual issue reporting through P04; returned-work consumption; department-safe specifications/history; truthful asynchronous behavior and verification requirements.
- **Dependencies**: Reuse P01 current identity/department authority, P02 stable order/piece identities and audit foundation, P03 submitted specifications/frozen terms/tracking, and P04 approval/release/issue/decision/reapproval rules. P06 owns quality inspection/rejection and creation of quality correction cycles; P05 consumes their eventual returned-work instructions. P05 normal transfer ends at quality incoming.
- **Client/release gates**: CAT-01/PRC-01 are confirmed and CAT-02 values received in the roadmap. - **Existing foundations**: Retain account/session safeguards, server authority, stable identities, current approval/issue rules, command reconciliation, frozen terms, safe projections, retained history and isolated verification infrastructure. Technical interfaces, storage, query/form and concurrency design belong to PLAN.

## User Scenarios & Testing _(mandatory)_

### User Story 1 — Find the correct department work (Priority: P1)

An eligible cutting or production employee finds current work and retained completed work without seeing another department's unrelated orders or private financial/contact data.

**Why this priority**: Every work action depends on an accurate, authorized piece and its current state.

**Independent Test**: Prepare approved pieces at several stages, open each existing queue/detail in two eligible sessions, and compare rows, counts, locations and history after reload.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** approved pieces released to cutting, **When** cutting incoming opens, **Then** their exact identities, retained specifications, actual source and readiness appear; production cannot start them yet.
2. **Given** one order with pieces in different states, **When** grouped/flat views and filters change, **Then** the same matching pieces and distinct order/piece counts appear without duplicated quantities or changed identity.
3. **Given** all department pieces have transferred onward, **When** an eligible department colleague opens completed/activity or its order/task detail, **Then** retained department work remains available through its history relationship, without action authority over pieces now elsewhere or access to unrelated orders.
4. **Given** wrong department, ineligible account, unknown order or stale explicit piece/group selection, **When** opened directly, **Then** a safe denied/missing/conflict state appears; no arbitrary first record, fixture or alternative piece is substituted.

### User Story 2 — Receive, start and continue colleagues' work (Priority: P1)

A worker receives and starts selected incoming pieces; another active colleague in the same department can continue them without an assignment transfer.

**Why this priority**: Department work must use real actors while preserving shared continuation.

**Independent Test**: Worker A receives/starts P1/P2; worker B continues P1 and later completes it. Reload and compare both actors and the unchanged P2.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** exact incoming pieces currently in the department, **When** receive-and-start succeeds, **Then** receipt/start and actual actor/time persist together, selected pieces become in progress and unselected pieces remain unchanged.
2. **Given** an existing reached separate receive/start entry point, **When** used, **Then** receipt alone does not claim work started; start records the actual worker/time. Overlapping controls express the same piece lifecycle, not parallel quantity records. Unreachable legacy dialogs need no new entry point.
3. **Given** A started P1, **When** eligible colleague B continues, completes or reports that piece, **Then** B is permitted without supervisor reassignment, A's original start remains traceable and B is recorded as the actual actor of B's action. No pause/resume or attendance subsystem is introduced.
4. **Given** stale/blocked/cancelled/wrong-department work, lost current eligibility or a failed write, **When** start/continue is attempted, **Then** no work/receipt/history is falsely confirmed. Repeated intent does not restart the clock or duplicate receipt/start.

### User Story 3 — Complete exact pieces and transfer them together (Priority: P1)

Staff finish a selected subset without waiting for every piece in the order, then see those same pieces incoming to the normal next department.

**Why this priority**: Partial manufacturing must preserve physical identity and quantity.

**Independent Test**: With P1/P2/P3 in cutting work, complete P1/P3 only; reload cutting/production, then start/complete those two in production and observe quality incoming.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** three in-progress cutting pieces, **When** P1/P3 are confirmed complete, **Then** those two complete cutting and enter production incoming together; P2 stays cutting/in progress. There is no completed-but-untransferred intermediate success.
2. **Given** selected production pieces in progress, **When** completion succeeds, **Then** they enter quality incoming, without quality acceptance, packing, warehouse receipt or delivery readiness.
3. **Given** any of SHOP, EXTERNAL, REPAIR, GIFT, READY, ACCESSORY or ADJUSTMENT, **When** ordinary work advances, **Then** cutting forwards to production and production to quality. Repair orders use the shared production incoming and in-progress queues.
4. **Given** empty, duplicate, stale, foreign or ineligible selected identities, **When** confirmation is attempted, **Then** the entire selected batch is rejected with no changes, including across orders, and invalid selection is explained without silently replacing/skipping pieces or claiming partial/full success.
5. **Given** concurrent completion/report/start or a lost completion response, **When** the result is reconciled, **Then** each piece has one accepted transition for that visit, no duplicated transfer/history, and no missing/created quantity.

### User Story 4 — Report one problem and rework only released pieces (Priority: P1)

A worker reports a problem on one current piece, then follows accepted instructions when approval or quality returns work.

**Why this priority**: An issue must stop only the affected piece and cannot bypass approval.

**Independent Test**: Report P1 while P2 continues; use P04 to return P1 to an allowed target, receive/start it, complete the normal route and inspect retained issue/work history.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** approved P1 incoming or in progress in the actor's current department with no unresolved issue, **When** a nonblank reason and optional notes are confirmed, **Then** P04 retains source state/department, reporter/time and issue; only P1 is held for approval. P2 can continue/complete.
2. **Given** a missing/blank reason, over-limit reason/notes, duplicate unresolved issue, wrong department, cancelled or already transferred piece, **When** reporting is attempted, **Then** visible validation/denial/conflict leaves work and issue history unchanged.
3. **Given** a resolved P04 return or positively approved sales response targeted to cutting/production, **When** that department opens returned work, **Then** accepted specifications, actual source, instructions and prior cycle are visible. The piece re-enters ordinary receive/start/work before completion.
4. **Given** a piece held for approval, owner sales contact or proposal review, **When** manufacturing reads it, **Then** the hold is explicit and work actions are unavailable; unapproved proposals are not executable specifications.
5. **Given** a later P06 quality correction targeted to cutting, **When** P05 work completes, **Then** the path is cutting → production → quality; a production-targeted correction follows production → quality. Prior cycles remain traceable. P05 does not create quality rejection or inspection decisions.

### User Story 5 — Recover truthfully from failures and concurrent work (Priority: P1)

Workers can distinguish confirmed work from pending, denied, stale or uncertain results without losing problem text or accidentally repeating a movement.

**Why this priority**: Multiple workers and unreliable responses must not corrupt work or mislead staff.

**Independent Test**: Delay/drop a result, change a selected piece in another session, switch account/resource, and retry/observe through existing screens.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** initial/background requests, actual empty data or a failed read, **When** rendered, **Then** loading, refresh, empty and error/retry are distinguishable; failure never becomes an empty-success result or demo data.
2. **Given** a pending confirmation, **When** double-click/Enter occurs, **Then** unintended duplicates are prevented; success/close/selection clearing follows confirmed results only. Ordinary cancel before sending leaves saved state unchanged.
3. **Given** recoverable failure or stale selection, **When** feedback appears, **Then** safe authored input remains available and refresh/review is offered; newer state is not silently overwritten and a stale selection cannot act on replacement pieces.
4. **Given** a response lost after commit or a pending dialog closed, **When** the worker returns, **Then** the original intent remains observable and reconciles to its actual outcome before any new effect. Closing does not imply rollback.
5. **Given** account/department/order/filter changes while requests are pending, **When** older results arrive, **Then** they do not expose private prior-context data, replace new input or announce another piece's success. Reconciliation still preserves committed work.

### Edge Cases

- One order spans departments and queues. Order counts count matching orders once per queue; piece counts count matching pieces once, without summing overlapping order/returned views into a false total.
- A returned piece is held versus released for correction. A historical issue is not a permanent hold, and a historical completion is not permission to act on its next visit or current downstream state.
- Cancelling a piece through P04 removes it once from active work; history remains. A fully cancelled order is not deliverable or 100% received. P05 cannot cancel, revive, edit prices or change paid.
- Selections outlive filtering, pagination, route changes or another worker's changes. An unreviewed hidden/stale ID cannot silently join or replace the confirmed set. No selection means no command.
- Batch spans orders, includes repeated IDs or contains a protected resource. Failure feedback must not disclose another department's unrelated order. Reject the entire selection with no changes, including otherwise eligible orders/pieces (D-004).
- Original starter changes department or is suspended; current colleague authority, retained original identity and current action identity remain separate. No worker-name impersonation is accepted.
- Completion, issue reporting, approved cancellation/return and current authority changes compete. No completed-and-held piece, lost companion update, duplicate visit or detached history can result.
- Date/duration/source/worker fields are absent in older records. Show honest absence rather than fake actors/times; elapsed duration requires actual timestamps and is not attendance/payroll. Calendar-dependent filters use explicit accepted configuration, not a fixed demo clock or computer timezone.

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Every protected read/write MUST use current eligible authenticated identity, role/department and resource relationship. Recheck mutable eligibility/state for changes. Cutting staff act only on current cutting work, production staff only on current production work; ADMIN has the same state-checked cross-department authority, attributed to ADMIN rather than an impersonated worker. Other departments, sales and approval gain no P05 work authority.
- **FR-002**: Reuse submitted order/piece identity, approved specifications, frozen terms, P04 release/issue/decision semantics and retained history. Browser actors/statuses/locations/counters/prices/timestamps are not authority. Reject unsupported authority fields; P05 changes no specifications, customer snapshot, money, order membership or business number.
- **FR-003**: Provide each department's dashboard, incoming, in-progress, returned/problem, completed/sent, activity, order/task detail and filters. Preserve department-root, waiting and sent aliases. Include repair orders in the shared production queues and their standard type filter. Redirect the retired repairs URL to the production incoming queue.
- **FR-004**: Incoming/ready contains active pieces released to the current department awaiting work, including released corrections; in-progress contains pieces currently working there. Held pieces cannot count as actionable incoming/in-progress. Each order row groups only its matching pieces, with exact current locations and eligible actions.
- **FR-005**: Returned/problem presentation MUST distinguish pieces currently held for an originating department's approval/contact/review issue from pieces actually released back for correction. Display source, instructions and relevant history without treating every historic issue/rejection as an active problem. Released corrections remain in the returned view while awaiting or doing that department's current correction visit, leaving on forward transfer or cancellation; earlier cycles remain in history. Released work follows normal receipt/start/complete eligibility; held work remains blocked.
- **FR-006**: Completed/sent represents retained successful department completion/forward-transfer for the latest applicable visit, not the mere fact that a piece is now downstream. Returning a piece for a new visit makes that visit pending/work again while previous completions remain in history. Active queue counts exclude cancelled pieces; cancellation and earlier visits remain visible in authorized history. Counts/search/type/status/pagination share department/filter scope, distinguish matching orders from pieces and do not multiply either by event/segment count.
- **FR-007**: Current safe order context and department-related unresolved issues remain readable under P04's current-resource rule. P05 additionally MUST support its required completed/activity/order/task reads through retained participation of that department in a piece's receipt/start/continuation/completion/transfer history. This limited historical relationship grants safe work/order context and retained department history, no new write authority or unrelated-order access. Unknown or invalid explicit order/piece/group references never fall back to another record. A derived group is not a separate business identity.
- **FR-008**: Receipt/start MUST operate on the exact confirmed incoming piece identities in the selected department. Combined receive-and-start records both facts coherently; reached separate controls distinguish received-awaiting-start from in progress without creating a second quantity ledger. Starting records the actual authenticated worker and server time. No new receipt-only screen or activation of unreachable legacy code is required.
- **FR-009**: Any currently eligible colleague in the same department MAY continue in-progress pieces and perform allowed completion/reporting regardless of the original starter. Preserve the original start and audit each actual subsequent actor/time; no exclusive assignee, supervisor approval, fabricated worker or reassignment workflow is introduced. Repeated start/continuation intent cannot reset the original start time or create duplicate history.
- **FR-010**: Reject receipt/start/continuation for wrong-department, unapproved, blocked, cancelled, already transferred or otherwise stale pieces. Recording a department receipt/start never means physical warehouse receipt or accepted order completion. Failed/denied actions leave saved work and history unchanged.
- **FR-011**: Partial/group completion MUST confirm the exact stable physical piece set, its department, count and normal destination. Only current in-progress eligible pieces may complete; unselected pieces retain work/location. Quantity alone, array order, first-N allocation or a segment fallback cannot choose authoritative pieces. Reuse existing piece selection/confirmation surfaces to make scope reviewable.
- **FR-012**: Completion and transfer MUST succeed together per accepted unit: cutting → production incoming; production → quality incoming. All seven types and correction visits follow that normal next step. Completing cutting never sends directly to quality; no special-operations department or repair/adjustment bypass is introduced.
- **FR-013**: Record each accepted piece completion/transfer with actual completing actor/time, source/target and retained visit/start history. Preserve the original starter when another colleague completes. Current displays of duration use actual recorded start/end facts, without fabricated clocks, erased prior visits or a timekeeping subsystem.
- **FR-014**: Confirmed selections MUST be nonempty, explicit, bounded and contain distinct order/piece identities. Validate membership, current department, allowed state and stale intent for every selected piece. Reject duplicates/foreign IDs rather than silently deduplicating, substituting or ignoring them. Exact wire limits and pagination are technical planning responsibilities; the existing 1–200 pieces per order bound is preserved without imposing an invented cross-order commercial limit.
- **FR-015**: A batch MUST accept or reject the entire exact confirmed selection as one outcome, including when it spans orders. If any selected piece is stale, foreign, blocked, duplicate or otherwise ineligible, or any required write fails, reject the whole batch without changes to any selected piece, work, movement or history. Do not commit an otherwise eligible order/subset, silently filter IDs or claim partial/full success. Explain safe failure context and require refresh/review before a fresh confirmation; observing/replaying an already committed batch recovers its single original result. No piece may complete without its corresponding transfer.
- **FR-016**: Retries/concurrent workers MUST not create duplicate receipt/start/continuation/completion/transfer/issues, lose unrelated work or partially retain required history. Same committed intent resolves to its actual single result; changed/stale intent yields a visible conflict and refresh/review. Disjoint valid pieces may progress without one worker overwriting another. Losing completion/report/cancellation/authority races cannot leave invalid piece/issue state. Unknown outcomes require observation of original intent before another effect.
- **FR-017**: Bind problem reporting to P04's existing exact-piece issue operation: approved current cutting/production piece incoming or in progress, no existing unresolved issue, cancellation or physical warehouse receipt. Require a nonwhitespace reason of at most 4000 characters; notes are optional at the same bound. Omitted/null/empty/whitespace-only notes mean no extra note; over-limit input is rejected without truncation. Actual reporter/source/state/time and hold persist together.
- **FR-018**: Reporting holds only the selected piece for P04 decisions; companion pieces remain eligible. P05 cannot decide A/B/C, resume held work, apply sales proposals or cancel pieces. Show safe instructions/status from the current issue and accepted decisions; preserve successive issues and reports without treating a resolved issue as still blocking.
- **FR-019**: Re-enter only actual released work at its accepted cutting/production target after P04 return/positive reapproval or later P06 correction. Preserve issue/correction origin, instructions and cycle history. P05 accepts and works these pieces normally, without inventing quality rejection, inspection, packing or approval commands. A P04 sales/contact/review hold never becomes actionable merely by opening a task route.
- **FR-020**: Piece state/history MUST be the single quantity authority. Group cards, batches, legacy segments, counts, locations and tracking are projections of those pieces. No operation creates/drops physical pieces, double-counts cancellation or independently advances segment quantities. Manufacturing progress/transfer never increments physically received warehouse count, accepted completion percentage or delivery readiness; zero active pieces never imply deliverable completion.
- **FR-021**: All cutting/production queue/order/task/specification/history/action/error output MUST omit unit/row prices, totals, paid/due/surplus, price provenance, private phone/email/address and raw financial/revision/proposal snapshots before delivery to factory staff. Show accepted manufacturing values and safe work instructions only; preserve original source labels, measurements and distinct floor-addition/final-additions fields. Do not grant factory staff P04 official financial printing or expose private data merely to hide it visually. ADMIN retains established broader context through its existing permitted surfaces; no new admin product surface is introduced.
- **FR-022**: Distinguish applicable initial loading, background refresh, actual empty, validation, pending, confirmed success, denied/missing, stale/conflict, read-error/retry and unknown-write outcomes. Success follows confirmed persistence, with exact outcome/count/target. No fixture fallback, silent local mutation or false empty/success is permitted.
- **FR-023**: Preserve authored problem text and safe selection context on recoverable failure; show field-associated errors and explicit refresh/review for stale state. Refetch does not erase dirty input or automatically rebase a stale confirmation. Pending conflicting triggers are disabled. Cancel before sending causes no write; closing after sending neither claims cancellation nor loses result observation.
- **FR-024**: Confirmed work synchronizes relevant department queues/counts, order/task details, locations/tracking, history/activity and next-department incoming reads. P05 supplies the real quality-incoming handoff; wiring quality work/inspection UI remains P06. Other sessions see the same persisted result after authoritative reload.
- **FR-025**: Account/department/resource/filter changes and late responses MUST preserve authority and selection boundaries. Hide obsolete private context, prevent old results resetting newer drafts or announcing another action, and still reconcile already committed intent. A historical read relation never grants action permission over downstream or newly returned pieces.
- **FR-026**: Preserve approved Arabic/RTL/Cairo presentation, routes, navigation, grouped/flat expansion/selection, tabs, accessible labels, keyboard/focus and narrow/wide usability. Necessary functional copy/state changes correct worker/repair/selection/privacy/optional-notes defects within existing surfaces. Failure messages follow the meaning/recovery matrix below in the active Arabic interface; technical artifacts remain English.

### Authority and Data Rules

| Operation                                             | Eligible cutting employee                    | Eligible production employee                    | Eligible ADMIN                                              | Other actors                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Current queue/detail/specifications                   | Current cutting relationship; factory-safe   | Current production relationship; factory-safe   | Authorized department context                               | No new P05 access                 |
| Completed/activity/task history                       | Retained cutting participation; factory-safe | Retained production participation; factory-safe | Authorized broader context                                  | No unrelated-order/history access |
| Receive/start/continue/complete/transfer              | Current eligible cutting pieces only         | Current eligible production pieces only         | Same state rules in selected department; actual ADMIN actor | Denied                            |
| Report problem                                        | Current eligible cutting piece, P04 rules    | Current eligible production piece, P04 rules    | Actual current piece/source rules                           | No new reporting department       |
| Resolve/approve/cancel/inspect/receive warehouse/paid | No P05 authority                             | No P05 authority                                | Only separate owning-phase operations                       | No new P05 authority              |

Authority is checked on saved current state, not supplied actor or old worker labels. Retain order/piece/visit/issue identities and original worker/actor snapshots. Work state, movement and required history succeed or fail together. Existing submitted specifications/frozen prices/contact snapshots, payment history and cancellations are preserved; work records are retained rather than erased on staff changes. List/search work is bounded and permission-scoped. PLAN defines exact interfaces and enforcement, not new product policy.

| Whole-batch failure                     | State that the entire selected batch could not be completed and no selected work was changed; explain a safe known cause | Refresh and review the exact selection before reconfirming; no partial success or inaccessible-resource disclosure       |

### Key Entities

- **Submitted Order / Physical Piece**: Stable upstream identity/type/approved specifications and individual current lifecycle. Groups/segments do not introduce extra pieces.
- **Department Work Visit**: Piece and department/source, receipt/start, original starter, subsequent actors/actions, completion/destination/times and relevant instructions. A correction can create another traceable visit without overwriting the earlier one.
- **Confirmed Piece Selection / Batch Result**: Exact selected order/piece identities, intended action/department and observed outcome; no new quantity authority. D-004 fixes one outcome for the entire selection; no partial commit.
- **Manufacturing Issue / Accepted Return**: Reused P04 affected piece/source/reason/optional notes/reporter/hold and accepted disposition/target; later P06 correction origin remains distinct.
- **Department Read / Activity Projection**: Authorized current or historical work, unique counts, safe specifications/location/source/target/worker/duration/history; excludes private factory data.
