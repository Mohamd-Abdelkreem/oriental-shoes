## User Scenarios & Testing _(mandatory)_

### User Story 1 — Find, receive and begin inspecting real pieces (Priority: P1)

An active quality employee or ADMIN finds production-completed pieces, receives/starts the selected inspection work, and lets a quality colleague continue without replacing the original start history.

**Why this priority**: Inspection decisions require real eligible quality work, correct identities and a consistent starting state.

**Independent Test**: With P01–P05 prerequisites satisfied, production transfers P1/P2 to quality incoming. Quality employee A starts P1, colleague B continues it, and another eligible session reloads the same saved location, workers and history while P2 stays incoming.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** eligible incoming pieces, **When** the actor starts the exact selection, **Then** those pieces enter quality inspection with actual actor/time and remain unreceived in warehouse; unselected pieces are unchanged.
2. **Given** A started P1, **When** active quality colleague B continues and later inspects P1, **Then** A's original start and B's actual actions remain traceable without reassignment or a second inspection start.
3. **Given** stale, cancelled, blocked, foreign-department or duplicate selected pieces, **When** start/continuation is attempted, **Then** the entire selection is rejected unchanged with refresh/review guidance.
4. **Given** unavailable data, a missing task or denied access, **When** a queue/detail is opened, **Then** the existing surface shows the applicable loading/error/empty/missing/denied state without selecting a different piece or inventing fixture work.

### User Story 2 — Accept and pack an exact inspected subset (Priority: P1)

Quality staff record accepted results, retain the packing information actually entered, and hand only accepted pieces to warehouse incoming.

**Why this priority**: Acceptance must preserve stock identity and packing evidence while keeping physical receipt separate.

**Independent Test**: Inspect P1/P2 from P1/P2/P3. Accept P1/P2 with carton count text `2`, carton references `C-12, C-13` and inspector note `Protect finished surfaces`. Reload quality accepted/history and the handoff facts; P3 stays in inspection and warehouse physically received count is unchanged.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** ordinary in-progress quality pieces with no previous problem or rejection, **When** the actor accepts a selected subset, **Then** one accepted result and warehouse-incoming handoff exist for each selected identity, with saved actor/time and entered packing/inspection notes.
2. **Given** the per-piece acceptance path has no packing inputs, **When** the actor accepts that piece, **Then** acceptance succeeds without invented carton values or a new mandatory packing form. Missing packing data is displayed honestly.
3. **Given** several inspections of an order, **When** later accepted pieces carry different packing values, **Then** both groups retain their own values and accepted-piece links; the earlier packing evidence is not overwritten or multiplied per piece.
4. **Given** a repeated command or lost response, **When** the result is recovered or the same accepted intent is replayed, **Then** no additional acceptance, movement, packing group or receipt is created.
5. **Given** any selected piece is no longer eligible or saving a required part fails, **When** the batch is confirmed, **Then** no selected result/handoff/packing change persists; safe input remains available for review/retry.

### User Story 3 — Reject, correct and reinspect without shortcuts (Priority: P1)

Quality staff reject exact pieces to cutting or production with reasons and instructions, follow the correction work, and record later reinspection and repeated cycles.

**Why this priority**: Correction must preserve identity, explain the work and return through the agreed manufacturing route.

**Independent Test**: Reject P1 to cutting for `Edge differs from approved specification` with `Recut edge to approved dimensions`, and P2 to production for `Sole bond is loose` with `Rebond and verify adhesion`. Complete normal correction routes and reinspect; reject P1 again, then accept it. Reload every cycle and compare companions and quantities.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an eligible quality-in-progress piece, **When** rejection supplies an allowed target, nonblank reason and nonblank correction instructions, **Then** that piece leaves inspection for target incoming and retains inspector/time, target, reason, instructions and its next correction cycle.
2. **Given** rejection to cutting, **When** the correction progresses, **Then** the route is cutting → production → quality. Rejection to production follows production → quality. No order type or corrective action bypasses these steps.
3. **Given** a corrected piece returns to quality, **When** staff start and record reinspection, **Then** it is linked to the originating correction cycle. Acceptance closes that cycle as accepted. A further rejection closes the prior cycle with its failed-reinspection outcome and opens exactly one new current cycle, retaining both histories.
4. **Given** missing/blank reason or instructions, an unsupported target, or a piece still outside quality, **When** rejection/reinspection is attempted, **Then** no decision, return or new cycle is saved and field/conflict feedback explains the correction needed.
5. **Given** P1 is under correction while P2/P3 remain eligible, **When** staff continue other work, **Then** only P1 is held by its correction; the order is not stopped wholesale.

### User Story 4 — Confirm mixed outcomes and escalate distinct problems (Priority: P1)

Quality staff inspect an explicit subset with both accepted and rejected results, or report an issue needing approval, without treating correction as approval or guessing which pieces a quantity represents.

**Why this priority**: The existing quantity/batch controls must preserve each piece's outcome and the established approval authority.

**Independent Test**: From five quality-in-progress pieces, explicitly inspect P1/P2/P3, accept P1/P3 and reject P2 to production; leave P4/P5 untouched. Separately report P4 through the approval issue path and show P5 still actionable. Repeat with a stale selected member and verify total rejection.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a selection from one or more orders, **When** accepted and rejected identities are explicitly confirmed, **Then** those disjoint outcomes cover exactly the inspected selection, and displayed quantities agree with the identified pieces. Common rejection details apply only to explicitly selected rejected pieces sharing those details.
2. **Given** a stale/duplicate/foreign/blocked member or invalid result data anywhere in a batch, **When** the batch is confirmed, **Then** the whole selection and all required effects are rejected unchanged, including across orders. No eligible-subset save, silent skip or substitute piece is allowed (D-002).
3. **Given** an incoming or in-progress quality piece needs an approval decision, **When** the actor reports a nonblank reason and optional notes, **Then** P04's issue path blocks only that piece and retains actual reporter/source/time. The command neither creates a quality rejection nor executes an approval/sales/cancellation decision.
4. **Given** a P04 issue/contact/reapproval hold, **When** a quality action is attempted, **Then** the hold remains until the accepted P04 release; opening a quality task or entering rejection details cannot bypass it.

### User Story 5 — Read consistent quality work and history safely (Priority: P2)

Quality staff use the existing dashboard, queues, order/task details and activity to follow work across departments, including prior inspections after handoff, without gaining financial or downstream write authority.

**Why this priority**: Operational follow-up must remain useful after pieces leave the current quality queue.

**Independent Test**: Transfer all pieces of an inspected order onward and reload its quality accepted, order/task and activity views. Check retained decisions/packing/correction history, consistent filters/counts, factory-safe specifications, aliases, another eligible session and forbidden direct access.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** pieces in different quality/correction/handoff states, **When** the same filters are applied in grouped and piece views, **Then** matching identities, totals and page metadata agree; a history flag does not falsely mark a resolved correction as current work.
2. **Given** every piece has left quality, **When** the actor follows an accepted/activity/order/task link for that department's retained work, **Then** the correct safe history remains accessible with no current downstream action. An unrelated order remains inaccessible.
3. **Given** a quality employee requests details, history, paper or an action result, **When** data is returned, **Then** it excludes financial and private-contact information and unapproved proposed specifications. Official finance-bearing printing is not granted to quality staff.
4. **Given** an action is pending, conflicts, fails or has an unknown result, **When** the actor stays, closes a dialog, changes filters or changes account, **Then** feedback remains truthful, safe drafts/results reconcile in the correct context and late replies cannot announce success for another account/piece.

### Edge Cases

- Zero, negative, fractional, excessive or inconsistent inspection quantities; no selected pieces; duplicated, unknown, cross-order-mismatched or overlapping accepted/rejected identities; a rendered page is not the full eligible selection.
- All accepted, all rejected, mixed outcomes, inspection of a strict subset, and distinct rejection targets/details handled through explicit per-piece selections rather than arbitrary quantity assignment.
- An ordinary piece with no earlier issue; multiple corrections on one piece; corrected work still in cutting/production; cancellation/approval hold or concurrent inspection invalidating confirmation.
- A colleague continues an existing inspection; double-click, repeated different requests for one decision, accept-versus-reject, report-versus-inspect, changed authority and required-save failure cannot duplicate or partially commit work.
- Packing omitted at a path without packing inputs, blank optional text, entered Arabic or mixed-language carton references, multiple packing groups and inspector notes, and a rejected-only result cannot invent accepted packing.
- An order appears in multiple piece-based queues; all pieces move onward; history-only access does not grant current actions; cancelled pieces remain in history but not active queues.
- Initial read failure versus an empty queue; unknown committed result versus known rejection; dirty dialog during refresh; late result after close/navigation/account/department change.

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Permit operational quality reads/actions only for authenticated, verified, active QUALITY employees and eligible ADMIN. Enforce current authority and resource/state eligibility at the action; reassignment/suspension immediately affects further business authority. Other departments and unapproved accounts cannot gain authority through a URL, hidden control or supplied identity.
- **FR-002**: Supply real dashboard, incoming, inspection, accepted, corrections, activity and order/task detail data. Preserve existing queue/status navigation, all-seven-order-type selection and free search by the fields already offered, including order, customer display name, model, piece reference and responsible worker. No new separate worker/date filter controls are required. Paginate lists/history, bound results and use stable ordering; matching counts cover the same authorized search/type/queue scope, not merely the rendered page.
- **FR-003**: Allow current quality work and limited retained quality-participation history after transfer. Detail/task routes identify the exact requested order/piece/work context; missing or inaccessible resources never fall back to the first order, another segment or another piece. Historical access grants no downstream action or unrelated-order read.
- **FR-004**: Apply the queue/count definitions below from the same physical pieces and retained decisions. Incoming/inspection/current correction counts exclude cancelled or held pieces from actionable work. Earlier rejection history alone never makes a resolved piece a current correction. Grouped orders can legitimately span queues; overlapping counts are not additive stock totals.
- **FR-005**: Receipt/start inspection moves exactly selected quality-incoming pieces to quality in-progress with actual starter/time and retained source history. It does not change warehouse receipt or delivery eligibility. Every reached receipt/start entry point uses these same semantics.
- **FR-006**: An eligible quality colleague may continue in-progress work and record decisions. Retain the original starter plus each actual later actor/time; do not require exclusive ownership, reassignment or a fictitious second start.
- **FR-007**: Inspection accepts one or more exact currently eligible quality-in-progress piece identities, including pieces with no prior issue. No final decision is allowed directly from incoming, through a still-active hold, or while a piece remains outside quality. Start is a separate observable prerequisite.
- **FR-008**: An inspection's accepted and rejected identity sets must be disjoint and cover exactly its explicitly inspected selection; untouched pieces stay outside that selection. Quantities are nonnegative whole-number counts of those identities, inspected count is positive and within eligible selected work, and accepted plus rejected equals inspected. Unsupported/duplicate identities, inconsistent counts and implicit first-N assignment are invalid. A batch may combine explicit differing outcomes; a shared rejection target/reason/instruction applies only to the selected pieces confirmed under it.
- **FR-009**: Per-piece, order/group, batch and task inspection entry points use the same eligibility/outcome rules and exact identities. Retain existing controls; make the intended task-result action reachable and route batch acceptance through the actual inspection/packing confirmation instead of a generic transfer bypass.
- **FR-010**: Acceptance records an accepted result and incoming-to-warehouse handoff together. It never sets physical receipt, warehouse completion, delivery-ready or customer-delivered state, nor increases received count or the physical-receipt completion percentage. No later P07 command is executed implicitly.
- **FR-011**: Persist the optional carton count text, carton reference text and inspector note actually entered in the reached inspection form, associated with that inspection's exact accepted group. Preserve original text/language and separate earlier packing groups; do not multiply one group's carton information per piece. Do not invent packing fields on a path without them, turn demonstration defaults into facts, impose numeric/unique carton rules, or let a later omitted/blank value erase earlier saved packing. Omitted/null/blank optional text means no additional recorded value for that result; entered values remain distinguishable from absence. A rejected-only result creates no accepted packing group.
- **FR-012**: Rejection requires explicit target CUTTING or PRODUCTION, a nonblank reason and nonblank correction instructions, each at most 4000 characters under the established manufacturing-text convention. The per-piece notes/instructions control supplies the instructions; no generic default substitutes for actual entry. Optional inspector notes use the same 4000-character maximum. Missing/null/blank required text, over-limit text or another target fails without truncation or changes. Packing stays free text; its finite input bounds are a technical planning decision, not approval of new carton business validation.
- **FR-013**: Each committed rejection records a retained correction cycle for that exact piece with inspector/time, target, reason, instructions, originating inspection and subsequent return/reinspection outcome. Repeated rejections increment that piece's cycle once per actual new rejection; repeated commands do not. Reinspection acceptance resolves the current cycle as accepted. Reinspection rejection ends the prior cycle with its failed-reinspection outcome and opens exactly one new current cycle together; a piece never has multiple current correction cycles. Retain all earlier rejection facts, cycle outcomes and notes rather than replacing them with the latest rejection.
- **FR-014**: A rejection opens target incoming work using P05 correction behavior. Cutting corrections follow cutting → production → quality; production corrections follow production → quality. Every order type, including repair and adjustment, follows this route. Corrected pieces re-enter quality incoming and require start/reinspection; no direct corrective shortcut, fabricated return or work from a P04-held proposal is allowed.
- **FR-015**: Reuse P04 manufacturing-issue reporting for exact quality-incoming/in-progress pieces requiring approval, with nonblank reason at most 4000 characters and optional notes at the same limit. Omitted/null/blank optional notes mean no extra note. Record actual reporter/source/time and one unresolved issue/hold per piece; other pieces continue. Quality rejection is a separate action; quality staff cannot execute approval decisions, sales changes/reapproval or cancellation. Consume an actual P04 release according to its target and normal route.
- **FR-016**: Reject client authority fields or attempts to alter approved specifications, frozen prices/customer snapshots, paid amounts, cancellations, inspector/worker identity, authoritative timestamps, received counts, delivery state or arbitrary piece locations. Quality acts through allowed operations only; entered text cannot grant authority.
- **FR-017**: Retain inspection starts/continuations, accepted/rejected results, packing/notes, correction returns/reinspections and issue links with stable order/piece references, actual actor/name/department at action time, authoritative timestamps and relevant before/after states. Later staff/profile changes or corrections never rewrite historical actors or earlier business facts. Report partial work without losing individual identities.
- **FR-018**: Every P06 selected batch, including receipt/start, continuation where offered, inspection/mixed accept-reject and issue reporting, commits the exact whole confirmed selection or rejects it unchanged. If any selected member/data is stale, duplicate, foreign, blocked, cancelled or otherwise invalid, reject the entire batch across all involved orders; refresh/review and explicit reconfirmation are required. Never silently omit members or partially commit an eligible subset (owner decision D-002).
- **FR-019**: Repeated/competing commands cannot produce duplicate starts, decisions, movements, packing groups, issues or correction cycles. Recover a committed original intent without repeating its effects; the same intent identity with changed input conflicts. Stale or losing competing actions expose a conflict and leave losing effects absent. Different requests for the same completed business action cannot bypass this rule.
- **FR-020**: Save each accepted operation's piece outcomes and required history/packing/correction/handoff effects together. Failure of a required part leaves the entire operation unchanged. Preserve unselected companions, prior work, frozen terms, quantities, received state and money on every denied/invalid/failed action. Do not label a lost response as proof of rollback.
- **FR-021**: Stable physical pieces remain the only quantity authority. Original, cancelled, active, quality-incoming/inspection, correction, accepted and physically received facts must reconcile without independent segment quantities, lost pieces or double cancellation deduction. Quality acceptance cannot itself yield 100% physical receipt or dispatch readiness; zero active pieces never imply a deliverable complete order.
- **FR-022**: Quality responses, including nested details, activity, paper, action outcomes and errors, exclude prices, totals, paid/balance/surplus, private contact/address, raw submitted snapshots and unapproved proposed specifications. Expose only accepted factory specifications and safe operational instructions/history. Quality employees cannot obtain official finance-bearing print; preserve the existing paper presentation using safe data and existing authorized print rules.
- **FR-023**: Preserve every route/alias in the surface table and retain safe accepted/correction/activity detail after all relevant pieces move onward. Counts, filters, grouped/flat rows, order/task details and shared tracking reflect the same saved facts after reload and in another eligible session. No converted read/action may fall back to browser fixtures/local success.
- **FR-024**: Show distinct loading, background refresh, true empty, missing, denied, read-error/retry, pending, confirmed-success and stale/conflict states. Prevent conflicting duplicate submits while pending. Announce success only after confirmed persistence; refresh affected quality/correction/next-stage handoff facts, order/piece tracking, counts and history without erasing unrelated drafts or falsely confirming downstream receipt.
- **FR-025**: Preserve safe inspection/packing/rejection input on recoverable validation/request errors. On conflict retain it for comparison but require refresh/review before another confirmation; do not silently rewrite selection/outcomes. Cancelling an unsent dialog writes nothing. An unknown write outcome remains visibly uncertain and recovers the original result before any new intent; closing a submitted dialog does not cancel an already accepted write or lose its recovery path.
- **FR-026**: Account/department/resource/filter changes and late responses must not publish another context's private data, overwrite a newer draft/selection or announce wrong-piece success. Session failure remains distinct from an operational read failure. No fabricated employee, fixed date or prototype role supplies authority.
- **FR-027**: Preserve approved Arabic/RTL/Cairo typography, layout, navigation, grouped/flat selection/expansion, tabs, dialogs, responsive behavior, accessible labels, keyboard/focus and field-error association. Add necessary function/state/privacy corrections within these surfaces. Errors convey the active Arabic interface's intended message/recovery below; this specification and other technical artifacts stay English.

### Authority and Data Rules

- Current eligible QUALITY staff act on current quality work; ADMIN retains agreed cross-department authority without bypassing state or validation. Cutting/production act on correction work through P05, approval/sales through P04, and warehouse receipt/delivery through P07.
- Retained quality participation supports safe history after handoff, not arbitrary order discovery or permission to mutate downstream pieces. Factory-safe output applies at the returned-data boundary, not solely through hidden cells.
- Piece identities and source labels/approved specification values stay stable through every cycle. Inspections do not edit or reprice submitted terms, erase cancellations, delete history or create extra pieces.
- Lists/search/history use finite pages and consistent authorized scope. Numeric capacity/latency targets, retention/deletion policies and production timezone decisions are not invented here.
- Required fields distinguish absent/null/blank from valid input as specified above. Optional text omission records no extra value; it is not a command to clear a prior retained fact. Unexpected fields cannot become privileged writes.
- No external provider action, physical warehouse acknowledgement or production release is part of a quality acceptance result. An interrupted response can leave a saved quality result uncertain until observed.

### Key Entities

- **Quality work context**: A stable piece's incoming/started quality visit, original starter, actual subsequent workers, current eligibility and normal/correction origin.
- **Inspection result**: Exact inspected identities and accepted/rejected outcomes, actual inspector/time and optional note; links to accepted handoffs or rejection cycles without a separate quantity authority.
- **Packing group**: Optional entered carton count/reference text and note associated with one inspection's explicit accepted-piece group, retained independently of later groups and physical receipt.
- **Quality correction cycle**: One piece's rejection, target/reason/instructions, normal manufacturing correction path, return/reinspection and resolution, retaining previous cycles.
- **Warehouse incoming handoff**: Accepted piece available for later explicit warehouse receipt; it carries no physical-received acknowledgement or delivery permission.
- **Manufacturing issue / activity**: Reused P04 approval escalation and retained actor/piece/time history; separate from ordinary quality correction.
